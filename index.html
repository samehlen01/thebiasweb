    What is The Bias?+

    The Bias is a wide-angle news outlet. Original articles bring the essential facts, major perspectives and uncertainty into one coherent read, with key claims traceable to the reporting behind them.

    What’s included with Premium?+

    The app is free to download, with limited reading and features. Premium includes unlimited reading, claim sourcing, International Outlook and The Rundown. See the current options in the App Store.

    Where can I get the app?+

    The Bias is available for iPhone and iPad. Get it from the App Store, or contact support@thebias.co.uk for help.
