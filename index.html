What is The Bias?

The Bias is a wide-angle news outlet. We publish original articles built from broad global coverage of the same event, bringing the essential facts, major perspectives and uncertainty into one coherent read.

Does “The Bias” mean unbiased?

No claim of perfect neutrality. Our approach is to make the reporting more transparent: show where accounts agree, where they conflict, and what remains unclear, so you can form your own view.

Is the app free?

The Bias is free to download, with limited reading and features. Premium unlocks unlimited reading, claim sourcing, International Outlook and The Rundown. You can see current subscription options in the App Store.

Where can I get it?

The Bias is available for iPhone and iPad. Download it from the App Store to start reading.
